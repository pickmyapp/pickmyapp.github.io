Best simple CRM for real estate agents (2026)

Quick answer

The best simple CRM for most real estate agents is Follow Up Boss: $69 per user per month ($58 on annual billing), built around fast lead follow-up with 200+ lead-source integrations and the cleanest broker reporting in the category. Solo agents on a budget should pick Wise Agent at $49 a month for up to five users — it covers contacts, drip campaigns, and transaction checklists in one flat fee. Brokerages that want a website, CRM, and lead generation in one quoted package should look at BoldTrail (formerly kvCORE). And if you want to spend $0, HubSpot's free CRM handles contacts and pipelines for up to two users.

What "simple" actually means for a real estate agent

Most CRM roundups rank features. For a real estate agent, the ranking should start somewhere else: does the lead get a call within five minutes, does the follow-up happen without you remembering it, and can you find the client's file while you are standing in their driveway?

A simple CRM for a real estate agent does four jobs well and gets out of the way on everything else. Lead capture: new leads from Zillow, Realtor.com, your website, and Facebook land in one place automatically, with the source attached. Speed to first contact: the system tells you who to call now, not who to call eventually. Drip follow-up: action plans and email/text sequences keep a lead warm for the months between first contact and a signed contract. Transaction tracking: checklists carry a deal from contract to close without a spreadsheet.

Everything else — IDX websites, AI scoring, dialers, marketing automation — is useful, but it is not what makes a CRM simple. A CRM with an IDX website you never use is not simple; it is expensive. The picks below are ordered by that test: how fast does a lead become a conversation, and how little setup does that take?

One more thing before the list: two names in this category changed in 2026. kvCORE is now BoldTrail, folded into Inside Real Estate's flagship brokerage platform, and Chime is now Lofty. LionDesk, a long-time budget pick, has been discontinued; its successor is Lone Wolf Relationships. Older guides still list the old names and old prices — if a review quotes kvCORE or Chime pricing, it is quoting a product that no longer sells under that name.

1. Follow Up Boss — best for teams

Price: Grow $69 per user per month, or $58 per user per month on annual billing. Pro is $499 a month for ten users ($416 annual), Platform is $1,000 a month for 30 users. Fourteen-day trial, no contract. The calling add-on is $39 per user per month ($33 annual) on Grow; unlimited calling and texting are included on Pro.

Follow Up Boss is a lead-conversion machine that happens to store contacts. Its job is getting every lead a fast, consistent response, and it is built around that one job more tightly than anything else in this list. New leads arrive from 200+ sources — Zillow, Realtor.com, Ylopo, BoldLeads, your IDX website, Facebook — and route to the right agent automatically, with the source, the property, and the lead's history attached. The action plans (drip campaigns) are battle-tested: text, email, and call tasks in a sequence that runs for months without you remembering it exists.

The broker reporting view is the cleanest in the category: who called, how fast, and which leads went cold. One 2026 test found average speed-to-first-call dropped 41% against a baseline drip workflow after switching to Follow Up Boss. That number is the whole argument — in real estate, the agent who calls first usually wins the client, and a CRM that makes the fast call the default is worth more than a CRM with more features.

Who should pick it: a team of 5–50 agents that already generates leads from portals and wants them worked properly. Who should not: a solo agent with ten leads a month — $69 a month for lead routing you do not need is money better spent on lead generation. Note the IDX gap: Follow Up Boss does not include an IDX website; you bring your own site and leads, and it handles the follow-up. If you need the website too, BoldTrail or Lofty is the all-in-one answer.

2. Wise Agent — best value for solo agents

Price: $49 a month, or $42 a month billed annually ($499 a year), for up to five team members on a shared login. Extra logins are $20 a month. Fourteen-day trial, month to month, no contract.

Wise Agent is the budget pick that does not feel like a budget pick. One flat fee covers contact management, drip campaigns, transaction checklists, a landing page builder, unlimited document storage, and a one-on-one onboarding session. It is the simplest CRM on this list to set up: most agents are running their first drip campaign the same afternoon, and the 24/7 live support is a real differentiator at this price — when your CRM breaks at 9 p.m. before a showing, someone answers.

The trade-offs are honest ones. Wise Agent has no IDX website of its own (it integrates with IDX providers instead), its lead routing is simpler than Follow Up Boss's, and its G2 rating (3.6/5 from a small review base) reflects an older interface rather than missing features. It is also priced per account, not per user, which is why the math works so well for a solo agent or a two-person team: $49 a month covers up to five people on a shared login, where Follow Up Boss would cost $345 a month for the same five seats.

Who should pick it: a solo agent or small team that wants a real CRM — contacts, drips, transactions — without paying team prices. Who should not: a brokerage that needs lead routing across dozens of agents and accountability reporting per agent; that is Follow Up Boss territory.

3. BoldTrail (formerly kvCORE) — best for brokerages

Price: quote only, three tiers, twelve-month minimum. Reported pricing starts around $499 a month plus setup. No free trial; demo only.

BoldTrail is Inside Real Estate's brokerage platform, built from kvCORE. It is not a CRM you buy; it is a platform your brokerage buys and gives to its agents. Every agent gets a website and CRM under the brokerage's brand, leads distribute across offices, and the accountability dashboards are designed for managing agent performance at scale. It bundles an IDX website, CRM, lead generation, AI behavioral lead scoring, and an AI text concierge, with 600+ MLS connections.

That completeness is the point and the problem. For a brokerage with 20–500 agents, one system that handles the website, the leads, and the follow-up is genuinely simpler than stitching five tools together. For a solo agent, it is the opposite of simple: a quoted price, a twelve-month minimum, and a platform built for managers, not for one person with a phone. If your brokerage already provides BoldTrail, use it — it is good. If you are choosing for yourself, do not buy it alone.

Who should pick it: a brokerage or large team that wants website, CRM, and lead generation in one system. Who should not: any solo agent, and any team that wants month-to-month billing or a published price.

4. Lofty, LionDesk's successor, and the $0 option

Lofty (formerly Chime) is the AI all-in-one alternative to BoldTrail: IDX website, CRM, lead generation, and AI automation in one quoted package, from around $449 a month reported. It leads the category on AI — automated lead nurture, AI-assisted follow-up, and smart lists that prioritize who to call. The same warning as BoldTrail applies: quote-only pricing and a brokerage-scale product. Worth a demo if you are comparing all-in-ones; not a pick for a solo agent.

LionDesk, long the affordable pick with built-in video email and video texting at $30 a month, has been discontinued. Its successor is Lone Wolf Relationships. If you find a guide still recommending LionDesk at $30 a month, it is out of date — check the Lone Wolf pricing page for the current product and price before deciding.

The $0 option: HubSpot's free CRM. HubSpot's free plan covers up to two users with contacts, companies, deals, and a visual pipeline, plus email logging and tasks. It has no real estate features — no IDX, no drip action plans, no transaction checklists — but it is a real CRM for $0, and for an agent with a handful of leads a month it is genuinely enough to start. The catch is the one every HubSpot user learns: the paid tiers jump fast (Starter from $7–$20 a seat, Professional $90 a seat plus a $1,500 onboarding fee), so treat the free plan as a starting point, not a forever home. If you are coming from a general CRM rather than a real estate one, our guides to the best free CRM for startups and the best CRM for freelancers and solo consultants cover the $0-to-cheap end of the market in more depth.

How to choose: three questions that decide it

1. How many leads a month, and from where? If leads come from Zillow, Realtor.com, and Facebook in volume, you need Follow Up Boss's routing and speed. If leads come from your sphere and past clients, Wise Agent's drips and transaction checklists matter more, and the $49 flat fee is the right size.

2. Solo or team? Solo agents should optimize for price and simplicity — Wise Agent, or HubSpot free to start. Teams of five or more should optimize for routing, reporting, and accountability — Follow Up Boss. Brokerages should compare the all-in-ones — BoldTrail and Lofty — on a demo, not on a feature list.

3. Do you need a website, or do you have one? Follow Up Boss and Wise Agent are CRM-only: you bring your own site and leads. BoldTrail and Lofty bundle the IDX website, which is simpler if you have neither, and more expensive if you already have both. Buying an all-in-one when you already have a website is paying twice for the same thing.

Whichever you pick, run the trial properly. Import fifty real contacts, not five. Set up one drip campaign end to end. Make a test call from the mobile app while standing outside, because that is where you will actually use it. A CRM that is simple in a demo and fiddly in a driveway is not simple. Frequently asked questions

What is the best simple CRM for real estate agents?

What is the cheapest good real estate CRM?

HubSpot's free CRM costs $0 for up to two users but has no real estate features like IDX or drip action plans.

It has no IDX, no drip action plans, and no transaction checklists, so once lead volume grows or you need automated follow-up, a real estate CRM like Follow Up Boss or Wise Agent earns its price in faster first contact.

Conclusion

Simple, for a real estate agent, means one thing: the lead gets a fast call and a consistent follow-up without you having to remember either. Follow Up Boss does that best for teams at $69 a user a month. Wise Agent does it best for solo agents at $49 a month flat. BoldTrail and Lofty do it for brokerages that want the website and the CRM in one quoted package. And HubSpot free does it for $0 while your lead volume is still small. Pick by lead volume and team size, not by feature count — run the trial with fifty real contacts, make a test call from the driveway, and keep the one that makes the fast call the default.
