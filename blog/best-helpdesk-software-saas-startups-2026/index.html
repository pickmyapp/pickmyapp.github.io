Best helpdesk software for SaaS startups (2026): 7 tools compared on real pricing

  • Updated: October 2026

Quick answer

For a 3-person SaaS startup, the cheapest real options in 2026 are <strong>Help Scout (free for up to 5 users)</strong>, <strong>Crisp Mini ($45/month flat for up to 4 seats)</strong> and <strong>Freshdesk Growth ($57/month for 3 agents)</strong>. At 10 seats, <strong>Crisp Essentials ($95/month flat)</strong> is the runaway value pick — Intercom Essential would cost $290 and Zendesk Suite Team $550 at the same headcount. Intercom and Zendesk fight back with startup programs: Intercom Early Stage is 93% off in year one, and Zendesk for Startups offers 6 months to 2 years free — but model the year-two renewal price before you commit, because that's where the sticker shock lives. And budget AI separately: in 2026 every vendor except Plain meters AI support by the resolution ($0.75–$2.00 each), which can exceed your seat bill.

What a SaaS startup actually needs from a helpdesk

Support tooling is one of the few purchases where startups routinely overbuy. Here's the honest filter before the tool-by-tool breakdown:

  • Email first, chat second. Vendors market chat-first, but in the early days 80%+ of tickets typically arrive via email. Email-ticketing strength matters more than the demo suggests.
  • A knowledge base is the cheapest agent you'll ever hire. The cheapest ticket is the one never filed. Every tool here except Tidio's low tiers and Crisp's free plan includes a help center.
  • AI is metered now. 2026 is the year every major vendor put AI support on a per-resolution meter (Intercom Fin $0.99/outcome, Help Scout AI Answers $0.75, Zendesk $1.50–$2.00, Tidio Lyro ~$0.70–$0.78, Freshdesk Freddy $49/100 sessions). Only Plain bundles customer-facing AI with no per-resolution fee. Estimate your resolution volume before signing.
  • Startup programs change the math. Intercom, Zendesk and Plain all run startup discounts that make premium tools near-free in year one. Always price years two and three.

If you're also weighing the chat-widget side of the stack, our Intercom vs Crisp comparison and Tidio vs Intercom comparison go deeper on the in-app messaging angle, and our Zendesk vs Freshdesk guide covers the enterprise ladder in detail.

The 7 tools, compared

1. Crisp — best flat pricing for small teams

Crisp prices per workspace, not per seat: Free ($0, 2 seats), Mini $45/month (4 seats), Essentials $95/month (10 seats), Plus $295/month (20+ seats). That makes 10 seats cost $95/month total — less than a third of Intercom Essential at the same headcount. You get a shared omnichannel inbox, built-in CRM, knowledge base and automation from Essentials up, plus Hugo AI with included credits (~450 conversations on Essentials; overages metered).

The laddering is the catch: the knowledge base, chatbot builder and Zapier/CRM integrations need the $95 Essentials tier, white-labeling needs Plus, and Mini/Essentials have fixed seat caps — you must upgrade tiers to add seats. For a chat-led SaaS under ~20 people, it's the cheapest credible path to a full support setup. Compare the two chat-first contenders in our Intercom vs Crisp for small business breakdown.

2. Help Scout — best email-first feel, genuinely free start

Help Scout feels like Gmail with superpowers rather than a ticketing system — a plus for SaaS startups that want support to feel personal. Pricing: Free ($0 for up to 5 users, 1 inbox, 1 Docs site), Standard $25/user/month, Plus $45, Pro $75 with a hard 10-user minimum ($750/month floor). The free plan covers a 5-person team indefinitely — the best free entry in this roundup.

AI Answers costs $0.75 per AI-resolved conversation (the cheapest per-resolution AI in the pack), with agent AI drafting included on Plus and Pro. Watch the Pro floor and the fact that SSO/SAML and HIPAA are Pro-only — a 6-person team with a security requirement can't buy them without paying for 10.

3. Freshdesk — the budget full helpdesk

Freshdesk is the value pick against Zendesk: Suite-like features at roughly half the price. Growth is $19/agent/month (annual) — the cheapest paid plan among the big four — then Pro $55 and Enterprise $89. Important correction to old advice: the free-forever plan is gone; what's offered now is a Free Program of $0 for up to 2 agents for 6 months only, after which you must upgrade.

AI is not included: Freddy Copilot is +$29/agent/month on Pro/Enterprise, and Freddy AI Agent sessions bill separately per 100 sessions. Growth + Copilot lands around $48/agent/month — still well under Zendesk. 800+ marketplace integrations and a genuinely unified email+chat+social inbox complete the picture. See our Zendesk vs Freshdesk for startups for the full ladder comparison.

4. Intercom — best in-app messaging, premium price

Intercom (now marketed as Fin + Intercom) remains the best in-app Messenger in the business — the right pick when in-app chat and onboarding-led growth are your product's front door. Seats run Essential $29, Advanced $85, Expert $132 per seat/month (annual); no permanent free plan, 14-day trial. Then the meters start: Fin AI Agent at $0.99 per outcome, Copilot for agents $29/agent/month, channels pay-as-you-go. One worked example: 10 seats on Advanced plus ~1,500 Fin resolutions/month lands around $31,500/year.

The startup program is the real story for early teams: Intercom Early Stage offers 93% off year one, 50% off year two, 25% off year three on Advanced (eligibility: under $10M raised, fewer than 15 employees). Two flags: Salesforce agreed to acquire Intercom for ~$3.6B in June 2026 (deal signed, not closed — roadmaps and pricing could shift), and some visitors have seen experimental pricing, so confirm list prices before budgeting.

5. Zendesk — the enterprise ladder

Zendesk is the pick for startups that expect to scale into a real multi-channel operation with SLAs and compliance: omnichannel everything, 1,000+ integrations, multilingual help centers from Growth, HIPAA on Professional. Pricing: Support Team $19/agent (email ticketing only — no chat or help center, so the real entry point is Suite Team at $55/agent), Suite Growth $89, Suite Professional $115, Enterprise custom. AI resolutions are included in small baseline allowances, then $1.50–$2.00 per resolution; Copilot for agents is +$50/agent/month.

Comparison sites report real bills running 40–60% above list once voice, AI and storage add-ons land, and it's overbuilt for a 2–5 person team. But Zendesk for Startups offers 6 months to 2 years free (eligibility: new customer, VC-backed angel to Series B, under 50 employees) — which makes the enterprise ladder free while you're small. Full detail in our Zendesk vs Freshdesk guide.

6. Tidio — cheapest for traffic-priced live chat

Tidio bills by conversation volume, not seats: Free ($0, 50 billable conversations/month, up to 10 seats), Starter ~$24–29/month (100 convos), Growth from ~$49–59/month (250–2,000 convos). For a very early, marketing-site-led SaaS with low ticket volume, the free plan covering 10 seats is remarkable. Lyro AI Agent is sold separately from ~$32.50/month for 50 AI conversations.

The catch is how fast the layers stack: Growth + Lyro + chatbot Flows + branding removal can approach ~$147/month quickly, reviewers flag confusing Flow-by-visitor metering, and terms allow auto-upgrades if you exceed plan usage. It's chat-first DNA, not a ticket-first helpdesk — great for PLG top-of-funnel, less so as your sole support system. Our Tidio vs Intercom comparison goes deeper.

7. Plain — best for Slack-native dev-tool SaaS

Plain is the specialist: Slack, Teams, Discord, email and chat in one native inbox, API-first, built for B2B and dev-tool SaaS where customers live in chat apps. Pricing: Foundation $35/seat/month (up to 5 seats), Horizon $299/month for 3 seats + $89 per extra seat (caps at 10), Frontier custom. The headline feature: customer-facing AI (Ari) with no per-resolution fees — pooled credits, unlimited on Foundation per Plain's TCO model — the only vendor here not metering AI by the resolution. Customers include Vercel, Cursor, n8n and Raycast, and the startup program is 50% off for VC-backed companies under $5M raised with 10 or fewer agents.

Limits: Horizon caps at 10 seats, advanced AI and SSO need custom-priced Frontier, and it's not built for B2C or phone/WhatsApp-first support. If your users are developers in Slack, nothing else here fits as naturally.

The metered-AI trap: budget this before you sign

In 2026, AI support went from bundled feature to metered utility at every major vendor. The per-unit numbers look small until you multiply by volume:

For busy teams, AI usage can exceed the seat bill, which is the single most common 2026 support-budget surprise. Set spending caps where the vendor offers them (Help Scout does), and re-estimate quarterly as ticket volume grows.

Startup programs: the fine print

Three programs genuinely change year-one economics:

  • Intercom Early Stage: 93% off year one, 50% off year two, 25% off year three on the Advanced plan — including 6 Advanced seats, 6 Copilot seats, 20 Lite seats, Proactive Support Plus and 300 Fin outcomes/month in year one. Eligibility: under $10M raised, fewer than 15 employees, new customer.
  • Zendesk for Startups: minimum 6 months free, up to 2 years free for up to 50 agents on some listings. Eligibility: brand-new customer, venture funding from angel to Series B, under 10 years old, fewer than 50 employees (250 if partner-referred).
  • Plain: 50% off eligible plans for VC-backed companies with under $5M raised and 10 or fewer agents.

Help Scout has no formal startup program — the free-for-5 plan is the de facto entry — and Freshdesk offers only trial and sales-rep discounts. The golden rule for all of these: sign for year one, budget for year three. The renewal cliff is where startups get hurt, so get the post-discount price in writing before you migrate your whole support history onto a platform.

Frequently asked questions

What's the cheapest helpdesk for a 3-person SaaS startup?

Help Scout's free plan (up to 5 users) and Tidio's free plan (50 conversations/month, up to 10 seats) both cost $0. The cheapest paid full helpdesk is Freshdesk Growth at $57/month for 3 agents, while Crisp Mini at $45/month flat covers up to 4 seats for chat-led teams. Intercom and Zendesk are far cheaper in year one via their startup programs (Intercom Early Stage is 93% off; Zendesk for Startups is 6 months to 2 years free).

Is Freshdesk really free?

Not anymore — the old free-forever plan is gone. Freshdesk now offers a Free Program: $0 for up to 2 agents for 6 months only, after which you must upgrade to a paid plan (Growth starts at $19/agent/month annually) and you cannot downgrade back. Any article still calling Freshdesk “free forever” is out of date.

Is Intercom worth it for an early-stage startup?

At list price, rarely — 10 seats on Advanced plus typical Fin AI usage can approach $31,500/year. Through the Intercom Early Stage program (93% off year one for startups under $10M raised with fewer than 15 employees), it's one of the best deals in support software. The catch: budget years two and three at 50% and 25% off respectively, and note Salesforce's pending ~$3.6B acquisition could shift roadmaps and pricing.

What does AI customer support actually cost in 2026?

Every major vendor except Plain meters it: Intercom Fin $0.99 per outcome, Help Scout AI Answers $0.75 per resolution, Zendesk $1.50–$2.00 per resolution, Tidio Lyro ~$0.70–$0.78 per AI conversation, Freshdesk Freddy $49 per 100 sessions. At 1,500 resolutions/month that's roughly $1,100–$3,000/month on top of seats. Plain bundles customer-facing AI with no per-resolution fee — the only vendor here to do so.

Which helpdesk is best for a Slack-native dev-tool SaaS?

Plain — it's built exactly for this: Slack, Teams, Discord, email and chat in one native inbox, API-first, with customers like Vercel, Cursor, n8n and Raycast. Foundation is $35/seat/month (up to 5 seats) with customer-facing AI included at no per-resolution fee, and the startup program takes 50% off for VC-backed companies under $5M raised with 10 or fewer agents. It's not built for B2C or phone-first support, so match it to a technical audience.

Conclusion

For most SaaS startups, the answer in 2026 is refreshingly cheap: Help Scout free or Crisp Mini gets a tiny team running for $0–$45/month, Freshdesk Growth is the cheapest full helpdesk at $57, and Crisp Essentials at $95 flat is almost unfair value at 10 seats. Reach for Intercom or Zendesk when — and only when — their startup programs make year one near-free and you've modeled the renewal. Whatever you pick, budget the AI meter separately from seats, because in 2026 the per-resolution bill is where support budgets go to die.
