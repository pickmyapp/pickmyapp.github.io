What Tobira Site Agent actually is

Tobira Site Agent gives your website its own AI agent — one that does not just answer questions, but closes the sale. Paste in your website address, Tobira scans your site in minutes — services, prices, blog posts, FAQs — and you add one badge to your site. From then on, two kinds of visitors can talk to it: humans on your website and their AI assistants — ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity, Copilot, Grok, Codex, and others.

The difference from a normal chatbot is what happens at the end of the conversation. A chatbot answers the question and points at a contact form. Tobira's agent asks about the visitor's case, suggests the right option, books the call, or sends a payment link — right inside the customer's own AI chat, without the customer ever opening your website. The founder's pitch is blunt: your customers already ask ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini to find businesses like yours, then ask the AI to book or pay — and your website was not built to answer that request.

It is built by Tobira (tobira.ai), whose first product was a network where AI agents find deals for their humans — launched in March 2026, also a #1 Product of the Day. The Site Agent is the second half of that idea: the network gives agents an address to reach each other; the Site Agent gives your business an agent that can be reached.

Why now: your customers' AI is doing the shopping

This product would have made no sense two years ago. A growing share of buyers — especially in B2B — now start a purchase by asking an AI assistant to research options, compare prices, and shortlist vendors. The human reads the AI's summary, not ten vendor websites. If your site cannot answer the AI's questions in a usable form, you are invisible at the exact moment the decision is made.

The industry calls this agentic commerce, and 2026 is the year every big platform started building for it. Sierra and Meta announced a Personal Agent Protocol on October 6, 2026; Google, Visa, and Mastercard have moved in the same direction. The protocols are still early — no production agent reads Sierra and Meta's October 2026 draft yet, by Tobira's own blog's account — but the direction is clear: the checkout is moving into the chat.

Tobira's own test, quoted on the launch post, claims AI got through sites with a Tobira agent 2.2 times faster and answered 10 out of 10 questions correctly, using 92 times fewer tokens than scraping a normal site. Treat both numbers as the vendor's own test, not independent verification. Our guide to AI agents explained covers the wider shift if you want the background first.

How Tobira Site Agent works, step by step

1. Paste your URL, then add your documents. Tobira scans your site — services, prices, team, blog — and writes the machine-readable documents AI agents look for: llms.txt, agent.json, and AGENTS.md. Then upload what the agent should answer from: price lists, PDFs, FAQs. Its knowledge base is built from your materials only — if the answer is not there, it says so and offers a call instead of guessing. That rule is the feature to test first, because a confident wrong price is worse than no answer. Setup takes about ten minutes, per the launch post; the vendor page says two.

2. Set your rules in plain words, then add one badge. "Give price ranges only," "never promise discounts" — you write the rules, the agent follows them. Private notes never leave: pricing is public, deal margins are private. Visitors chat with your agent on your site; visiting AI agents find its public address and talk to it directly. People see your usual website; AI agents get a version made for them, with the same facts in structured form.

3. Leads come to you. Outcomes land where you already work: Telegram, WhatsApp, email, or your CRM — a demo booked, a trial link sent to a CTO's Claude agent, an order paid through Gemini. Every conversation is tagged with its source ("via ChatGPT," "via Claude") and outcome. The agent also has a voice mode, speaks the visitor's language (English, Spanish, German, French, Portuguese, Japanese, Arabic, Russian), and hands hard cases to your team with full context. Your agent also gets a public address in the Tobira network (tobira.ai/@yourbusiness), where 800+ agents can find it and check the fit before bringing you a deal.

Tobira Site Agent vs a normal website chatbot

The comparison that matters is not Tobira vs another AI tool. It is Tobira vs the chatbot already on your site — because that is the purchase it replaces.

The row that decides it is the third one. A chatbot optimizes your website; Tobira optimizes the conversation that happens before anyone reaches it. If your buyers still arrive as humans browsing pages, a chatbot is cheaper and simpler. If a meaningful share arrive as an AI doing research, the chatbot cannot even see the customer. Who Tobira is actually for — and who should wait

You are the target customer if: you sell a service with a price list and a booking step — SaaS, agencies, consultants, clinics, B2B services — and your buyers already research through ChatGPT or Claude before they contact you. The vendor page names SaaS first: buyers compare tools with AI, and the agent answers on seats, integrations, and SSO, then books the demo or sends the trial link. If one booked demo a month is worth more than $49 to you, Lite is a cheap test.

Try the free plan first if your AI traffic is unproven. It runs on your own key, so the cost is just a few dollars of model usage, and you can measure how many visitors actually arrive through AI before paying a monthly fee. Run it for a month: if "via ChatGPT" and "via Claude" are a trickle, you have your answer.

Wait if your business runs on phone calls, walk-ins, and referrals, and your website is a brochure rather than a sales channel. A Site Agent cannot create AI demand that does not exist for your market; it can only catch the demand already arriving.

Risks, limits, and what to check before you trust it

1. It is one day old. Tobira Site Agent launched November 6, 2026; the Product Hunt page shows a single review, rated 5 out of 5. There are no independent case studies and no published customer results — the network behind it dates to March 2026, also young. Treat the 2.2x and 10-out-of-10 claims as the founder's test results: ask the agent your ten hardest customer questions and check every answer against your price list.

2. Hallucination is a pricing risk. The vendor's rule — offer a call instead of guessing — is exactly right, and the claim most likely to fail in practice. An agent that invents a discount or quotes the wrong tier creates a customer-service problem that costs more than the sale. Test it with wrong-premise questions ("your $19 plan includes SSO, right?" when it does not) before going live.

3. The network is small and the protocols are unfinished. Eight hundred agents is a beginning, not a marketplace, and standards like Sierra and Meta's Personal Agent Protocol are still drafts no production agent reads yet. Tobira runs its own network — fine today, but you are betting on one company's network, not an open standard.

4. Conversation caps are the hidden bill. Lite's ~300 conversations a month sounds generous until a long chat eats 20 replies, and a popular post could burn a month's allowance in a week. The vendor page does not spell out overage pricing — ask what happens at the cap before a campaign drives traffic to the agent.

5. Read the privacy terms. Tobira says data is handled under GDPR, with a data processing agreement on paid plans, and that private notes never leave. A DPA on paid plans means the free plan's terms deserve a read — confirm where the model runs and what the provider retains first.

How to start with Tobira this week

Start free and measure before you pay. Sign up at tobira.ai, paste your URL, and let it scan your site. Add your price list and one FAQ document, write three rules in plain words (what it must never promise, what it must never quote, when it must hand off to a human), and connect your own AI key so the trial costs only model usage. Then test it like a customer: ask your ten hardest questions from ChatGPT and Claude, two with a false premise, and check every answer. Book the free demo call and ask who in your industry is already on the network, and what happens when you hit your conversation cap. If a week of real traffic produces booked calls or orders, Lite at $49 is an easy yes.
