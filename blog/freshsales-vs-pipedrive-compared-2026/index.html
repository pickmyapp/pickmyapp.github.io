Freshsales vs Pipedrive Compared (2026)

  • CRM Software
  • Published Nov 5, 2026
  • Updated: November 2026
  • 12 min read
  • By PickMyApp Research Team
Quick answer

Freshsales and Pipedrive cost exactly the same at five users — $195 a month at the working tier — but buy different things. Freshsales is the better pick for a small team that wants phone, email, chat, and AI scoring in one tool without add-ons, plus a free plan for up to 3 users. Pipedrive is the better pick for a team that lives in its pipeline and wants the fastest adoption, the most integrations, and forecasting from the $39 tier. Never compare them by plan name: Freshsales Growth is $9 and Pipedrive Growth is $39.

Freshsales and Pipedrive are the two CRMs small sales teams most often shortlist, and comparing them by their plan names is a trap. Freshsales Growth costs $9 per user a month; Pipedrive Growth costs $39. Freshsales Pro costs $39; Pipedrive has no Pro at all. The names mean nothing. The honest comparison is the price of a five-person team at the tier that actually does the work — and there the two products cost exactly the same: $195 a month. What differs is what that $195 buys. Freshsales is the better pick for a small team that wants phone, email, chat, and AI scoring in one tool without add-ons; Pipedrive is the better pick for a team that lives in its pipeline and wants the fastest adoption, the most integrations, and the cleanest forecasting. What is Freshsales?

Freshsales is the sales CRM from Freshworks, built for small and growing sales teams that want contact management, a visual pipeline, and built-in phone, email, and chat in a single tool. Freshworks says more than 74,000 businesses use its products, and Freshsales grows into the same vendor's Freshdesk and Freshservice — a real path for a team that will later need support tooling.

Freshsales sells three paid plans: Growth at $9 per user per month billed annually, Pro at $39, and Enterprise at $59. Growth covers a Kanban pipeline, contact lifecycle stages, built-in chat, email and phone, email templates, custom fields, and basic workflows — but only a single pipeline, no sales sequences, no Freddy AI insights, and no custom reports. Pro adds contact scoring, deal insights, custom sales activities, territory management, sales sequences, and multiple pipelines. Enterprise adds forecasting insights, field-level permissions, custom modules, a sandbox, and audit logs. There is also a free plan for up to 3 users, but it is not shown on the pricing page itself — it appears on a separate plan-comparison page — and it is thin: contacts, accounts, deals, one pipeline, two-way email sync, templates, built-in phone, and a chatbot builder, with no workflows, no API access, no file storage, no sales dashboard, and no Freddy AI insights.

The number to remember is the jump: Growth to Pro is 4.3 times the price per seat, from $9 to $39, because sequences, lead scoring, and multiple pipelines all sit behind that one step. Pro is the tier most sales teams actually need, which makes $39 — not $9 — the real entry price for a team that automates anything.

What is Pipedrive?

Pipedrive is a pipeline-first sales CRM built around a visual Kanban board, activity-based selling, and fast adoption: deals move across stages, every deal has a next activity, and the tool nags you until it is done. Pipedrive says more than 100,000 sales teams use its CRM, and it is consistently rated the faster of the two to adopt.

Pipedrive renamed its plans in 2026, and this is the single most useful fact in this guide: most comparison articles still quote the retired names — Essential, Advanced, Professional, Power, Enterprise — with stale prices. The current self-serve tiers, read from the vendor pricing page in November 2026, are Lite at $14 per seat per month billed annually, Growth at $39, Premium at $59, and Ultimate at $79. Lite covers lead, calendar, and pipeline management, AI-powered report creation, 500-plus integrations, and meeting intelligence. Growth adds full email sync with open and click tracking, bulk email, automations and nurturing sequences, subscriptions and forecast reports, a meeting scheduler, and a contacts timeline. Premium adds LeadBooster lead generation and routing, custom scoring and company data enrichment, AI-powered multi-email tools and shared team inboxes, contracts and e-signatures, and deeper customization. Ultimate adds fortified account security, phone and email data enrichment, a sandbox testing account, extended phone support, and partner discounts. There is no free plan — only a 14-day free trial with no credit card and unlimited trial users.

Pipedrive's steps are gentler than Freshsales' — $14 to $39 to $59 to $79 — but the features a team actually needs are spread across more tiers and paid add-ons, which is where the real cost hides. Our Zoho CRM vs HubSpot comparison shows the same pattern in a different pair of tools.

Freshsales Pro and Pipedrive Growth cost exactly the same at five users — $195 a month — and Freshsales Enterprise and Pipedrive Premium also match at $295. But the feature mapping is not equal, so "same price" does not mean "same CRM." Freshsales Pro includes lead scoring and sequences; Pipedrive Growth includes sequences and automations but no lead scoring, no e-signatures, and no shared team inbox — those need Premium. A manager who lives in forecasts gets them on Pipedrive Growth at $39; on Freshsales, forecasting insights are Enterprise-only at $59. Price the plan where the features you need become included, not the plan whose feature list you happen to be reading.

Features compared

Automation and sequences

Both tools gate automation, but at different doors. Freshsales Growth includes basic workflows — 20 are reported in the vendor feature matrix — and Pro adds advanced workflows; sequences start at Pro ($39). Pipedrive Lite includes zero automations: any team that automates a single task starts at Growth ($39), where the cap is reported as 50 active automations in most October 2026 reads, though one source says 30 — treat the exact number as unverified and confirm it on the vendor page. Sequences on Pipedrive are capped by plan: 5 on Growth, 25 on Premium, 50 on Ultimate. Freshsales sequence limits were not verified in this research pass and are not quoted here.

AI

Freshsales' Freddy AI does contact scoring, deal insights, and email drafting, but scoring and insights are Pro-gated, and the customer-facing Freddy AI Agent chatbot is a metered add-on at $49 per 100 sessions, with 500 sessions granted once per account on paid plans — a one-time grant, not a monthly allowance. Pipedrive's AI Sales Assistant, which writes and summarizes emails, creates reports, and offers deal guidance, is available from Lite, with custom scoring on Premium and meeting intelligence (pre-meeting briefs and call transcripts) on Lite. Neither bundles unlimited AI on the entry tier, but Pipedrive puts its AI in front of a cheaper buyer.

Ease of use and integrations

Pipedrive is the faster tool to adopt. Its pipeline-first design, deal-rotting alerts, and activity-based selling give a new rep a clear next step for every deal, and its learning curve is consistently rated the shorter of the two. Freshsales is a broader suite CRM: more capability per dollar at the low end — phone, chat, and email built in — but more to learn, and its vendor's development attention is split across Freshdesk and Freshservice. On integrations, Pipedrive claims 500-plus apps against Freshsales' roughly 200-plus in third-party comparisons. Pipedrive is the better hub for a team that assembles its own stack; Freshsales is the better all-in-one for a team that wants to buy one thing and start selling.

Phone, email, and the add-on trap

The phone gap is the most concrete difference between these two CRMs, and the one most comparisons understate. Freshsales includes a phone dialer, chat, and email on every plan — but call credits are a separate prepaid purchase from $5, and free incoming local minutes are capped at 2,000 a month on Growth, 3,000 on Pro, and 5,000 on Enterprise, after which incoming local calls cost $0.013 a minute. Pipedrive has no built-in phone at all: calling needs a third-party integration such as CloudTalk or JustCall, and full email sync only starts at Growth. A team that calls all day must budget a phone tool on top of Pipedrive's seat price; a team on Freshsales must budget call credits and watch the minute caps.

Then there is the add-on trap, which flips the ranking. Pipedrive sells LeadBooster, Smart Docs, and Projects as add-ons — about $32.50 a month each for the first two — and includes all three on Premium and Ultimate. A five-seat team on Growth ($195 a month) plus LeadBooster and Smart Docs ($65 a month) pays $260 a month — close to Premium's $295, which includes both plus more automations, reports, and fields. Freshsales has its own version: branded documents cost $19 per user per month, more than double the Growth seat price, so a five-user team that needs them adds $95 a month. Freshsales also carries a contract quirk: under the Freshworks master agreement, you may not reduce your user count or downgrade your plan during a subscription term. Pipedrive's switching costs are different — per-plan caps on automations, reports, custom fields, and leads/deals — and neither vendor publishes a one-click importer for the other, so migration means rebuilding pipelines, sequences, and automations by hand.

Which should you choose?

Choose Freshsales if

  • You want phone, email, and chat built into the CRM, not bolted on.
  • You have 1–3 users and want a genuinely free starting plan.
  • You need lead scoring and sequences at the $39 working tier.
  • You expect to add Freshdesk support tooling under the same vendor later.

Choose Pipedrive if

  • You want the fastest adoption and a pipeline your reps actually update.
  • You live in forecasts and want them from the $39 tier, not $59.
  • You assemble your own stack and want 500-plus integrations.
  • You want AI assistance and meeting intelligence from the cheapest paid tier.

The deciding question is not which CRM is better — at five users, the working tiers cost the same dollar. It is which set of compromises fits your team: Freshsales' 4.3-times price jump and capped phone minutes, or Pipedrive's add-on stack and missing dialer. Run both trials with the same five deals and the same launch sequence, and price the plan where your features become included.

Frequently asked questions

Which is cheaper, Freshsales or Pipedrive?

At the door, Freshsales: Growth is $9 per user per month against Pipedrive Lite at $14, and Freshsales has a free plan for up to 3 users while Pipedrive has none. At the working tier, they cost exactly the same — Freshsales Pro and Pipedrive Growth are both $195 a month for five users billed annually. The cheapest headline is not the cheapest workable plan on either side.

Does Freshsales have a free plan, and what are its limits?

Yes, a free plan for up to 3 users, shown on Freshworks' plan-comparison page but not on the pricing page itself. It covers contacts, accounts, deals, one visual pipeline, two-way email sync, templates, built-in phone, and a chatbot builder — but no workflows, no API access, no file storage, no sales dashboard, and no Freddy AI insights.

Does Pipedrive have a free plan?

No. Pipedrive offers a 14-day free trial with no credit card and unlimited trial users, but there is no free tier. If you need a free CRM, Freshsales' 3-user free plan or the options in our best free CRM guide are the honest starting points.

Does Pipedrive include a built-in phone dialer?

No. Pipedrive has no native phone; calling needs a third-party integration such as CloudTalk or JustCall. Freshsales includes a dialer on every plan, but call credits are a separate prepaid purchase and free incoming local minutes are capped by plan.

How many automations does Pipedrive allow per plan?

Lite allows zero automations. Growth is reported at 50 active automations in most October 2026 reads, though one source says 30 — confirm the number on the vendor page. Premium allows 150 and Ultimate 250. Freshsales Growth includes basic workflows (20 reported) and Pro adds advanced workflows; Freshsales sequence limits were not verified and are not quoted here.

Conclusion

Freshsales vs Pipedrive is not a price competition — at five users, the working tiers cost the same $195 a month, and the full-featured tiers cost the same $295. It is a trade-off between two philosophies: Freshsales the all-in-one, with phone, email, chat, and AI scoring built in and a free plan for tiny teams, at the cost of a 4.3-times jump to Pro and capped phone minutes; Pipedrive the pipeline specialist, with faster adoption, cheaper forecasting, and 500-plus integrations, at the cost of add-on pricing and no dialer. Pick the one whose compromises you can live with, trial both with real deals, and price the plan where your features become included.
