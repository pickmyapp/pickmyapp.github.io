Best Helpdesk Software for Ecommerce (2026)

Quick answer

For Shopify-only brands, Gorgias ($10–$900/month by ticket volume) has the deepest native integration. Amazon, eBay, or Walmart sellers should pick eDesk ($39/agent/month). Lean teams get the best per-seat value from Re:amaze ($29/agent/month) or Freshdesk ($19/agent/month), and Tidio's free plan covers the smallest stores. Large operations pick Zendesk ($55+/agent/month); AI-first scaling brands choose Richpanel ($99/seat/month). AI add-ons are billed separately everywhere — budget roughly $0.70–$2.00 per automated resolution on top.

A helpdesk is the single inbox where every customer question about your store lands: emails, live chats, Instagram DMs, Amazon buyer messages. An ecommerce-specific helpdesk goes further by pulling order data into the conversation — what the customer bought, where the shipment is, whether a refund is owed — without opening three other tabs.

The split that matters most is the billing unit: some tools charge per agent, others by tickets or conversations, and AI resolutions are a separate line item almost everywhere. What makes an ecommerce helpdesk different

Generic helpdesks route messages. Ecommerce helpdesks resolve orders. The differences that matter:

  • Order data inside the ticket. The best tools show orders, tracking status, and refund eligibility next to the message — and let agents edit orders or issue refunds without leaving the inbox.
  • Marketplace message handling. Amazon and eBay hold sellers to response-time SLAs; marketplace-built helpdesks pull those messages in natively and track the SLA per channel.
  • Pre-purchase chat. Live chat on product pages answers sizing and shipping questions that would otherwise become abandoned carts.
  • Ecommerce macros. "Where is my order?", return labels, and damaged-item claims are template-able workflows — a tool's macro and rule quality decides how much of this runs itself.

Gorgias — best for Shopify DTC brands

Gorgias is built almost entirely around Shopify, and it shows in the one feature no general helpdesk matches: agents can view and edit a customer's order, issue a refund, or resend tracking without leaving the conversation. It also attributes revenue to support conversations — many DTC brands treat it as a profit lever rather than a cost center.

Pricing is the catch, and it is unusual: Gorgias bills by ticket volume, never by seats, so you can add unlimited agents for free. As of October 2026: Starter $10/month (50 tickets), Basic $60 ($50 annual, 300 tickets), Pro $360 ($300 annual, 2,000), Advanced $900 ($750 annual, 5,000), plus $0.36–$0.40 per extra ticket. The AI Agent add-on runs roughly $0.90–$1.00 per automated interaction — and AI-resolved tickets also count against your ticket allowance.

Native integration covers Shopify and Shopify Plus, plus BigCommerce. WooCommerce is not supported, and Amazon or eBay messages need the third-party ChannelReply app. Best for Shopify-only DTC brands with real ticket volume — skip it if most sales happen on marketplaces.

Re:amaze — best for chat-first small stores

Re:amaze leads with live chat and messaging — web chat, Facebook, Instagram, and SMS land in one inbox — and its AI agent is included from the entry tier rather than gated behind a premium plan. It is a simpler, calmer tool than Zendesk, aimed at small and mid-size stores.

Pricing is flat per seat with no ticket caps: Starter at $59 a month covers up to 500 conversations; per-agent plans run Basic $29 ($26.10 annual), Pro $49 ($44.10), and Plus $69 ($62.10). Shopify connects natively with order lookup, and BigCommerce and WooCommerce are supported; there is no native Amazon, eBay, or Walmart coverage.

Best for small stores where live chat carries most of the volume and you want per-seat pricing over per-ticket metering. One caveat: the interface gets cluttered once email volume climbs, so email-heavy teams should look at Freshdesk instead.

Tidio — best free start for tiny stores

Tidio combines live chat, email, and social messaging with Lyro, its AI agent, and Flows, a visual chatbot builder — and its real strength is the entry point. A genuinely free plan covers 50 billable conversations a month with live chat and ticketing, enough for a brand-new store to prove chat converts before spending anything.

Paid plans start at Starter, $29 a month ($24.17 billed annually) for 100 conversations; Growth starts at $59 ($49.17 annual) for 250–2,000; Plus starts at $300 with custom volume. Lyro AI is sold separately — from $39 a month for 50 AI conversations, about $0.78 each, falling to roughly $0.70 at higher volumes — so budget the AI quota on top of the base plan.

Best for tiny stores that want live chat today at the lowest possible cost. If live chat is the whole strategy, our Intercom vs Crisp comparison for small business weighs the dedicated chat tools against each other.

eDesk — best for multi-marketplace sellers

eDesk is the only tool on this list built for sellers who trade on marketplaces first. It connects Amazon, eBay, Walmart, Etsy, TikTok Shop, and 300+ other channels natively — order and tracking data sits next to the message in the ticket, with no bridge app and no extra bill. It also tracks marketplace response SLAs per channel, which matters when Amazon is grading you.

Pricing is flat per agent with unlimited tickets: Essential $39 a month, Growth $89, Professional $119, with roughly 20% off on annual billing. The AI Agent is included on every tier and billed at $0.99 per conversation it actually resolves. One pricing wrinkle: the $39 entry tier covers a single store or marketplace, so a seller on three channels steps up to Growth quickly.

Best for sellers on two or more channels who want marketplace messages and order data in one place. For Shopify-only stores, it is overkill — multichannel infrastructure you never touch.

Freshdesk — best budget omnichannel for lean teams

Freshdesk is the value play: full multichannel ticketing from a major support vendor, with seats far cheaper than Zendesk. The classic line covers email and ticketing; Freshdesk Omni adds live chat, phone, and messaging on the same seat.

Pricing, billed annually: classic Growth $19 per agent a month, Pro $55, Enterprise $89; Omni $29, $79, and $119. A free program covers up to 2 agents for 6 months. Freddy AI Copilot is a $29 per agent a month add-on on Pro and Enterprise only, and the Freddy AI Agent gives 500 free sessions once per account before charging $49 per 100 sessions.

Shopify and WooCommerce connect through the app marketplace rather than natively, and Amazon or eBay need third-party apps too — this is a general helpdesk at an ecommerce-friendly price, not an ecommerce-native tool. Freshdesk is the best pick for a lean team that wants real ticketing, SLAs, and automation rules at the lowest per-seat price. Expect add-on costs if you want the full AI experience.

Zendesk — best for large, complex operations

Zendesk is the deepest and most customizable platform here: skills-based routing, granular reporting, multi-department workflows, and an app marketplace of more than 1,500 integrations. That depth is also the warning label — setup is measured in weeks rather than hours, and it genuinely needs an admin to configure well.

Pricing is per agent, billed annually: Support Team $19 a month (email ticketing only), Suite Team $55 — the real entry point with messaging, live chat, and a help center — Suite Growth $89, Suite Professional $115; Enterprise is quote-only. AI Copilot is $50 per agent a month extra, and AI agents bill $1.50–$2.00 per automated resolution. There is no permanent free plan.

There is no native Shopify integration — it runs through a third-party app — and the AI is general-purpose rather than trained on ecommerce. Zendesk is the best pick for large operations with dedicated support admins or multi-brand setups. A small store will pay for depth it never uses.

Richpanel — best for AI-first self-service at scale

Richpanel is built around a different thesis: deflect tickets before they reach a human. Its AI agent, trained on your store's policies, answers routine questions; a self-service portal handles returns and tracking lookups; agents step in only for the exceptions. It advertises a 50% automation guarantee aimed at scaling brands.

Pricing sits near $99 per seat a month on annual billing, plus roughly $0.20 per AI conversation — one of the cheaper per-resolution AI rates in this roundup. That math favors stores where AI handles most of the repetitive queue: the seat price buys the humans, the metered AI buys the automation.

Integration is Shopify-first, with returns and tracking actions; marketplace coverage outside Shopify is limited. Best for fast-growing DTC brands whose ticket volume is outrunning headcount.

Verdicts by store size

Match the tool to your ticket volume, not to the feature list.

  • New store, under ~100 tickets a month: start free with Tidio, or Gorgias Starter at $10 — a chat widget and an inbox, not a platform.
  • Small team, 2–5 agents, chat-heavy: Re:amaze Basic at $29 a seat — predictable, no ticket caps.
  • Lean team wanting cheap ticketing: Freshdesk Growth at $19 a seat, stepping to Omni for chat and phone.
  • Multi-channel seller: eDesk from $39 a seat — marketplace SLAs are not optional.
  • Scaling DTC, 1,000+ tickets a month: Gorgias Pro at $360, or Richpanel to have AI absorb the growth instead of hiring.
  • Large or multi-brand operation: Zendesk Suite Professional and up, with an admin to run it.

Verdicts by sales platform

Where you sell decides the shortlist faster than any feature comparison.

  • Shopify-only: Gorgias for depth, Richpanel for AI-first deflection, Re:amaze for budget chat. Still choosing a storefront? See our Wix vs Squarespace comparison for small business.
  • WooCommerce: Re:amaze or Tidio connect natively; Freshdesk works through its app marketplace.
  • Amazon, eBay, Walmart sellers: eDesk — nothing else here handles marketplace messages natively.
  • Shopify plus Amazon plus social: eDesk, the only option here that treats the marketplace as a first-class channel.

How to choose: inbox, automation, and self-service

Shared inbox vs. true ticketing

A small team can live in a shared inbox — Re:amaze and Tidio work this way. Once you need SLAs, routing rules, and CSAT scores, you need true ticketing: Freshdesk Pro, Zendesk, or Gorgias at volume. Buy for today, but pick a vendor whose ticketing tier you can grow into.

Automation and AI macros

Repetitive questions — "where is my order?", return labels, damaged-item claims — should be macros or rules, not typed replies. AI agents now answer a share of these outright, but every vendor bills AI separately: roughly $0.70 per Lyro conversation on Tidio, $0.90–$1.00 per Gorgias interaction, $0.99 per eDesk resolution, $1.50–$2.00 per Zendesk resolution. Model AI volume as its own line item.

Self-service widgets

A knowledge base and FAQ widget deflects a meaningful share of tier-1 questions before they become tickets, at no per-conversation cost. If you sell cross-border, check multilingual support — Freshdesk Pro, Re:amaze, and eDesk all cover it; entry tiers often do not.

Watch the billing unit: per-seat pricing is most predictable for small teams; per-ticket pricing (Gorgias) is cheap at steady volume and punishing when a product goes viral. Read the overage terms — that is where helpdesk bills surprise people.

Frequently asked questions

What is the cheapest helpdesk software for a small ecommerce store?

Tidio's free plan ($0 for 50 conversations a month) is the cheapest start. For paid ticketing, Freshdesk Growth at $19 per agent per month (billed annually) and Gorgias Starter at $10 a month for 50 tickets are the lowest entry points — though Gorgias bills by tickets, not seats.

Does Gorgias work with WooCommerce?

No. Gorgias has no native WooCommerce integration — its deep order-level integration is built for Shopify and BigCommerce. WooCommerce stores should look at Re:amaze or Tidio, which both connect natively, or Freshdesk through its app marketplace.

Which helpdesk is best for Amazon and eBay sellers?

eDesk is the clearest fit: it connects Amazon, eBay, Walmart, Etsy, TikTok Shop, and 300+ other channels natively, with order and tracking data inside each ticket. Essential starts at $39 per agent per month. Gorgias and Zendesk reach marketplaces only through third-party apps.

Is a free helpdesk enough for a new store?

Usually yes for the first few months. Tidio's free plan covers 50 conversations a month with live chat and ticketing, and Freshdesk offers a free program for up to 2 agents for 6 months. Upgrade when you need SLAs, automation rules, or a second agent — or when ticket volume outgrows the free limits.

How much should an ecommerce store budget for customer support software?

A solo store can start at $0–$30 a month with Tidio or Gorgias Starter. A team of 2–5 agents typically spends $60–$300 a month on per-seat tools like Re:amaze or Freshdesk. Stores handling 1,000+ tickets a month usually land between $300 and $900, budgeting AI add-ons ($0.70–$2.00 per resolution) separately.

Conclusion

There is no single best helpdesk for ecommerce — there is a best one for your channels and volume. Shopify-only DTC brands: start with Gorgias. Marketplace sellers: start with eDesk. Small teams wanting predictable per-seat pricing: compare Re:amaze and Freshdesk. Brand-new stores: prove the value of chat for free with Tidio. Whatever you shortlist, run the AI add-on math separately — the base plan is only half the bill.
