How We Research

Every guide follows the same four rules, so you can trust what you read.

  • [01]

    We check vendor pricing pages

    Every price in a guide comes from the vendor's own page on the date we publish. We note the date.

    Where a free plan exists, we sign up and use it, so we can tell you where the limits really bite.
