About PickMyApp

  • Updated: October 2026
  • By PickMyApp Research Team

Who we are

PickMyApp is an independent software guide publisher. We write in-depth comparisons, pricing explainers, and how-tos for business software — workflow automation, CRM, email marketing, project management, web hosting, and AI tools.

We are reader-supported through advertising, not vendor payments. No company can pay for a ranking, a review, or a recommendation on this site.

How we research

Every guide follows the same four rules:

  • We check vendor pricing pages. Every price comes from the vendor's own page on the date we publish, and we note that date.
  • We test free tiers. Where a free plan exists, we sign up and use it, so we can tell you where the limits really bite.
  • We update guides regularly. Pricing and features change; we review guides on a schedule and show the last-updated date.
  • No paid placements. Vendors cannot buy coverage.
