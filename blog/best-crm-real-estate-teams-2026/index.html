Best CRM for real estate teams (2026)

  • CRM Software
  • Published Nov 7, 2026
  • Updated: November 2026
  • 12 min read

Quick answer

The best CRM for most real estate teams is Follow Up Boss: $69 per user per month on Grow ($58 annual), or $499 a month flat for ten users on Pro, built around the one job that decides team revenue — getting every internet lead a fast, tracked first call, with round-robin routing, lead ponds, and broker dashboards. Brokerages that want the IDX website, CRM, and lead generation in one quoted package should compare BoldTrail (formerly kvCORE) and Lofty (formerly Chime), both quote-only, reported from around $449–$499 a month. Small teams on a budget can run Wise Agent at $49 a month flat for up to five users, and HubSpot's free CRM covers two users for $0 — but neither is a real estate CRM.

What makes a team CRM different from a solo-agent CRM

A solo agent's CRM has one job: remind one person to follow up. A team's CRM has five jobs, and the solo CRM fails at four of them. Lead routing: a new lead must reach the right agent in seconds, not sit in a shared inbox until someone claims it. Speed-to-lead accountability: the broker has to see who called, how fast, and which leads went cold — research published in Harvard Business Review found companies that contacted a lead within an hour were nearly seven times more likely to qualify it. Fair distribution: round-robin and lead ponds stop the loudest agent from taking every lead. Transaction coordination: deals pass between agents and coordinators, and the checklist must survive the handoff. Onboarding: a new agent has to be productive on day one.

Those five jobs are the test for every product in this guide. One note on scope: this guide is about teams — five agents and up, or a brokerage. Solo agents should read our companion guide to the best simple CRM for real estate agents. Two names changed recently: kvCORE is now BoldTrail, Chime is now Lofty, and LionDesk was discontinued in 2025.

1. Follow Up Boss — best for lead routing and accountability

Price: Grow $69 per user per month ($58 on annual billing). Pro is $499 a month for ten users ($416 annual); Platform is $1,000 a month for 30 ($833 annual). Fourteen-day trial, no contract, no setup fee. Calling is a $39-per-user add-on on Grow ($33 annual); unlimited calling and texting are included on Pro and Platform.

Follow Up Boss is the only CRM in this guide built from the ground up for teams. New leads arrive from 200+ sources — Zillow, Realtor.com, Facebook, your IDX website — and route automatically by rules, round-robin, or lead ponds. The action plans (drip campaigns) run text, email, and call tasks in sequence for months. The broker reporting view is the cleanest in the category: who called, how fast, and which leads went cold, per agent.

The catch is the calling add-on. Grow ships without built-in calling or texting, and calling is not optional for a team that lives on internet leads. Add the $39-per-user dialer and a five-agent team on Grow pays $540 a month — $41 more than Pro's $499 flat rate for ten users, calling included. If your team has five or more agents who call leads, skip Grow and price Pro. One more thing to know: Zillow Group acquired Follow Up Boss in December 2023, and a November 2025 privacy-policy change lets Zillow use data from mutual customers — worth reading before a team hands its database to a Zillow-owned CRM.

2. BoldTrail and Lofty — best all-in-one platforms for brokerages

BoldTrail (formerly kvCORE) — price: quote only, three tiers, twelve-month minimum. Reported from around $499 a month plus a setup fee near $999, rising to $999–$2,500+ a month for teams. No free trial; demo only. Lofty (formerly Chime) — price: quote only, four tiers (Agent, Team, Broker, Enterprise). Reported from around $449 a month, Premier near $700 a month for 15 users, Enterprise near $1,500 a month for 100 users. Setup fees reported from $500 to $2,000 by tier.

BoldTrail and Lofty are not CRMs you buy; they are platforms your brokerage buys and gives to its agents. Both bundle an IDX website, CRM, lead generation, and AI — BoldTrail with 600+ MLS connections and an AI text concierge, Lofty with AI lead scoring and its Homeowner Agent (launched April 2026), which scans contacts for seller intent.

That completeness is the point and the problem. For a brokerage, quote-only pricing is normal — you negotiate, you get onboarding, and the per-agent cost drops sharply at scale (BoldTrail reportedly below $25 a month per agent for large teams). For a five-agent team, it is the opposite of simple: a quoted price, a twelve-month minimum, a setup fee, and a platform built for managers. Neither vendor publishes a price, and third-party guides estimate real-world totals at 1.5–3x the reported starting price once add-ons and ad spend are counted. BoldTrail vs Lofty: BoldTrail is the safer pick for a large brokerage that wants the deepest MLS coverage and a platform used by RE/MAX, eXp Realty, and Berkshire Hathaway HomeServices. Lofty is the pick for a team that wants the most aggressive AI. Compare them on the demo, and get the setup fee and per-agent cost at your headcount in writing before you sign.

3. Wise Agent and HubSpot — the budget and free routes for small teams

Wise Agent — price: $49 a month, or $42 a month billed annually ($499 a year), for up to five team members on a shared login. Extra logins are $20 a month. Fourteen-day trial, no contract.

Wise Agent is the budget pick that does not feel like one: one flat fee covers the whole account. Contacts, drip campaigns, transaction checklists, and a landing page builder are all included. The trade-offs are honest ones: no IDX website of its own (it integrates with IDX providers instead), simpler lead routing than Follow Up Boss — no ponds, no round-robin, no per-agent dashboards — and a G2 rating of 3.6/5 that reflects an older interface rather than missing features. The shared-login model also weakens per-agent accountability: five people on one account is not five seats.

HubSpot free CRM — price: $0 for up to two users and 1,000 contacts, with contact and company records, a deal pipeline, email logging, and tasks. Starter is $20 a seat a month at list ($7 promo for new customers). Professional is $90 a seat plus a $1,500 onboarding fee. HubSpot is not a real estate CRM: no IDX, no drip action plans, no transaction checklists, no lead routing rules — a team that buys it as one will spend the first month building what Follow Up Boss includes. What it does offer is a real CRM for $0 and a platform that scales from two users to two hundred. For a team of two or three that mostly works its sphere and past clients, HubSpot free — or Starter at $20 a seat — is enough to start, and Starter sequences substitute for Follow Up Boss's action plans at a third of the price. The catch: the paid tiers jump fast, and the free plan's two-user limit forces a paid seat for the third teammate. Three conclusions. First, the Follow Up Boss crossover is at five agents: Grow with calling costs $540 a month for five; Pro costs $499 flat for up to ten, calling included. How to choose: three questions that decide it

1. Where do your leads come from? If leads come from Zillow, Realtor.com, and Facebook in volume, you need Follow Up Boss's routing and speed. If leads come from your sphere and past clients, Wise Agent's drips and transaction checklists matter more, and the $49 flat fee is the right size. If you have no lead source at all, BoldTrail or Lofty bundles one in — but budget for the ad spend.

2. How many agents, and how fast are you growing? Two to five agents on a budget: Wise Agent, or HubSpot free. Five to fifty agents on internet leads: Follow Up Boss Pro at $499 flat. Twenty agents and up, or a brokerage: compare BoldTrail and Lofty on a demo, and get the per-agent cost at your headcount in writing.

3. Who is accountable for follow-up? If the answer is "the broker, with numbers," you need per-agent reporting — Follow Up Boss, BoldTrail, or Lofty. If the answer is "each agent, on their own," Wise Agent's flat plan is enough. If the answer is "nobody yet," fix that first: a CRM does not create accountability, it only measures it.

Whichever you pick, run the trial properly. Import fifty real contacts per agent, not five. Set up one drip campaign end to end. Walk one deal from contract to close with a handoff between two agents. For general CRMs, see our best free CRM for startups and HubSpot vs Pipedrive for small teams guides.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best CRM for a real estate team of 10 agents?

Follow Up Boss Pro at $499 a month flat for ten users ($416 annual), with unlimited calling and texting included. It has the best lead routing (round-robin, ponds, rules) and the cleanest per-agent reporting, and no contract. A ten-agent team on Grow with the calling add-on would pay $1,080 a month — more than double Pro.

How much does a real estate CRM cost per agent?

It depends. Follow Up Boss Pro works out to $49.90 per agent at ten users and $33.33 at thirty on Platform. Wise Agent is $9.80 per agent at five users and $17.45 at twenty, because it charges a flat $49 a month. BoldTrail and Lofty are quote-only but reportedly drop below $25 per agent at brokerage scale. HubSpot Starter is $20 per seat, but it has no real estate features.

Is Follow Up Boss or BoldTrail better for teams?

Follow Up Boss is better for teams of 5–50 agents that already have a lead source and a website: published pricing, no contract, and the best lead routing and reporting. BoldTrail is better for brokerages of 20+ agents that want the IDX website, CRM, and lead generation in one quoted package with a twelve-month minimum.

Does Wise Agent work for teams, or only solo agents?

Wise Agent works for small teams of up to five on one $49-a-month flat fee, extra logins at $20 a month — a ten-agent team pays $149 a month. It covers contacts, drip campaigns, and transaction checklists, but its lead routing is basic and its shared-login model gives weaker per-agent accountability than Follow Up Boss. Best budget pick for small teams; not the pick for twenty agents on internet leads.

Can a real estate team use HubSpot instead of a real estate CRM?

A team of two or three working its sphere can start on HubSpot's free CRM ($0 for two users) or Starter ($20 a seat) — sequences substitute for drip campaigns. But HubSpot has no IDX, no drip action plans, no transaction checklists, and no lead routing rules, so a team on internet leads will spend its first month building what Follow Up Boss includes. Use HubSpot as a starting point, not as a real estate CRM.

Conclusion

A team CRM is not a solo CRM with more seats — it is a routing and accountability machine, and the price that matters is the price per agent at your headcount. Follow Up Boss Pro at $499 a month flat is the best answer for most teams of 5–50 agents. BoldTrail and Lofty are the right call for brokerages that want the IDX website and lead generation bundled in. Wise Agent at $49 a month flat is the best budget pick for small teams, and HubSpot free is a $0 start for a team of two. Pick by lead source and team size, not by feature count — and keep the CRM that makes the fast first call the default.
