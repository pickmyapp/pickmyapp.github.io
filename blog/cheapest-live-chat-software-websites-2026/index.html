Cheapest Live Chat Software for Websites in 2026: 8 Budget Picks

  • Published Oct 14, 2026
  • Updated: October 2026
  • 11 min read
  • By PickMyApp Research Team
Quick answer

Tawk.to is the cheapest live chat software for websites: genuinely free with unlimited agents and unlimited chats. Chaport is the best cheap paid option (free for 2 operators, Pro at $29/month), Tidio is the best value free-plus-AI start ($29/month Starter with chatbot flows), and Crisp is the best flat-rate pick for teams ($45/month per workspace, unlimited conversations). Avoid headline prices: most tools charge per agent, and AI chatbot fees are almost always billed separately.

Live chat is one of the few website features that pays for itself quickly: visitors with questions get answers instead of bouncing, and support costs drop compared with phone or email. But the pricing pages are a maze of per-agent fees, conversation quotas and AI add-ons. This guide cuts through them. Every price below was checked against vendor pricing pages and current 2026 reviews on October 14, 2026. We cover the eight cheapest serious live chat tools for websites, what free tiers actually include, how per-agent pricing multiplies your bill, and the hidden chatbot costs that catch budget shoppers by surprise. If you want a deeper head-to-head on two of the mid-range picks, read our Tidio vs Intercom comparison and our Intercom vs Crisp comparison.

How live chat pricing really works

Live chat vendors use three pricing models, and the model matters more than the headline number. Per-agent (per-seat) pricing multiplies the listed price by every person who logs in. Flat per-workspace pricing is one price no matter how many conversations you have. Conversation-based pricing caps how many chats you get each month, with overages or forced upgrades beyond the limit.

Do the math for a realistic 4-person support team. LiveChat Team at $49 per agent per month (annual billing) costs $196 a month — $2,352 a year. Crisp Essentials at $95 per workspace costs $95 a month flat, for up to 10 seats. And Tawk.to costs $0, no matter how many agents you add. The headline prices look close; the real bills are worlds apart.

Quick fact: a 5-agent team pays roughly $245/month on LiveChat Team ($49/agent), $150/month on Olark Standard ($29/agent monthly), $95/month on Crisp Essentials (flat, up to 10 seats), and $0 on Tawk.to (unlimited agents).

Tawk.to — best truly free

Tawk.to is the only live chat tool here that is completely free at the core: unlimited agents, unlimited chats and unlimited websites, with ticketing, a knowledge base, WhatsApp, Messenger and Instagram included. There is no plan ladder, no per-agent fee and no conversation quota. The company monetizes through optional add-ons, all priced per property (per website) rather than per person.

The add-ons are where a bill can appear: removing the "Powered by Tawk.to" badge costs $29 per month per site, AI Assist (AI answering from your help center) costs from $29 per month, and voice add-ons start at $49 per month. Tawk.to also sells hired human chat agents from $1 per hour. A realistic budget setup on one site with the badge removed and AI Assist costs $58 per month — still less than two agents on most per-seat tools.

Trade-offs: Tawk.to does not support WhatsApp, Instagram, Telegram or SMS as chat channels beyond its basic integrations, and support runs mostly through a community forum and the same chat widget you are evaluating, so response times can lag on edge cases.

Chaport — best cheap paid plan

Chaport is a clean, chat-first widget that gives away more on its free plan than most rivals: 2 operators, unlimited chats and websites, and 60-day chat history. It also has web, desktop and mobile apps and solid integrations with WordPress, Shopify, HubSpot and Zapier.

The paid plans are flat-rate: Pro starts at $39 per month ($29 on annual billing) with 4 operators, auto-invitations, messenger integrations (Facebook, Viber, Telegram), pre-chat forms and an autoresponder. The $129 per month ($99 annual) Unlimited plan adds chatbots, a knowledge base with an FAQ bot, teams and assignment rules. Chaport's automation is rule-based, not generative AI — keep that expectation set.

Quick fact: Chaport's free tier covers 2 operators while most competitors' free tiers cover 1 — meaningful if two people answer chats.

Tidio — best free-plus-AI start

Tidio combines live chat, email, Messenger and Instagram in one inbox, with a free tier covering 50 conversations a month and up to 10 seats. The paid entry point is Starter at $29 per month ($24.17 annually) for 100 conversations, and Growth starts at $59 per month ($49.17 annually) from 250 conversations — but the price climbs in steps as conversation volume grows, reaching up to $349 for 2,000 conversations.

Tidio's real budget trap is its three-meter model: the plan price covers human conversations only. The Lyro AI agent is billed separately from $39 per month for 50 AI conversations, and Flows automation starts at $29 per month. A Growth account with both add-ons runs around $127 per month — more than double the headline price. That said, Lyro reads your knowledge base and product data to answer questions naturally, which is a genuine step up from 2020-era rule-based bots, and Tidio's Shopify integration makes it a favorite with small ecommerce stores. Our Tidio vs Intercom comparison digs into how it stacks up against the premium option.

Crisp — best flat-rate team plan

Crisp charges per workspace instead of per agent, which makes it the easiest tool here to budget for a growing team. Plans: Free, Mini at $45 per month per workspace (up to 4 seats, unlimited conversations), Essentials at $95 (up to 10 seats), and Plus at $295 (up to 20 seats). AI automation credits are included on paid plans — roughly 90 automated conversations on Mini — rather than sold as a separate add-on.

Watch the plan jumps: Crisp's cost steps between tiers rather than climbing smoothly with headcount. At 3 agents it is $45/month; at 8 agents you land on Essentials at $95/month; at 15 agents you are on Plus at $295/month. For a small team, it is competitive; past 20 seats, per-agent tools or open-source options can undercut it. Our Intercom vs Crisp comparison models the exact crossover point.

Olark — best classic per-agent chat

Olark is the veteran of this list (founded in 2009): simple live chat with cobrowsing, real-time visitor insights, targeted chat rules and strong reporting, billed per agent. Standard costs $29 per agent per month, dropping to $23 on annual billing and $19 on a two-year subscription. A quiet free plan (1 agent, 20 chats a month) kicks in automatically after the 14-day trial if you do not upgrade.

AI chatbots and advanced automation are gated to the custom-priced Pro plan, so Olark at its cheapest is a pure human-chat tool. It is a solid pick if you want dependable chat with an accessible widget (WCAG 2.1 AA certified) and do not need AI or multichannel extras. If you later outgrow chat-only support, our Zendesk vs Freshdesk comparison covers full helpdesks for startups.

Pure Chat — best simple small-business chat

Pure Chat, now a Ruby brand, is built for US small businesses that want straightforward live chat with minimal setup. There is no permanent free plan — just a 30-day trial of Pro. Growth starts at $49 per month ($39 annually) with 4 users, 1 website, unlimited chats and 100 SMS notifications; additional users cost $13-$15 each. Pro starts at $99 per month ($79 annually) with 10 users and unlimited websites.

Pure Chat is easy to set up and integrates with WordPress, Shopify, HubSpot and Zapier, but the automation is basic compared with Tidio or Crisp at similar prices. The per-additional-user fees also stack at team scale: a 15-user team on Growth would pay around $182 per month on annual billing. It makes sense for a small team that values simplicity and a US vendor over feature depth.

LiveChat — best polished (with a catch)

LiveChat has the most polished pure-chat agent experience in the industry, and its entry price looks tempting: Starter is $25 per agent per month ($19 annually). The catch: Starter is limited to one user. Any team of two or more needs Team at $59 per agent per month ($49 annually), and 5 agents on Team costs $245 per month — nearly $3,000 a year.

There is no free plan, only a 14-day trial. AI assist (reply suggestions, summaries) is bundled in all tiers, but a real AI chatbot requires a separate ChatBot subscription starting at $52 per month. LiveChat is the best pick here if chat quality and agent experience matter more than absolute cost — but it is "cheap" only for solo operators.

Intercom — the premium ceiling

Intercom is not cheap, and it is on this list as the reference point that shows what "expensive" looks like. Essential starts at $39 per seat per month ($29 annually) with zero free seats — every person touching the platform needs a paid seat. Its AI agent Fin costs $0.99 per resolved conversation on top of seats, so a team with 500 AI resolutions a month pays nearly $500 extra on top of seat fees.

Intercom earns its price for product-led SaaS companies with complex needs, but a 5-agent team pays roughly $145/month on seats alone before AI. If that bill scares you, one of the tools above will serve you at a fraction of it — start with our Intercom vs Crisp comparison to see the savings side by side.

The hidden costs nobody lists up front

Budget shoppers get caught by the same four traps. Per-agent multiplication: always multiply the headline price by your team size — $19/agent/month sounds cheap until 8 agents make it $152/month. AI add-ons billed separately: Tidio's Lyro, LiveChat's ChatBot and Intercom's Fin are all separate line items; only Crisp and Chaport's Unlimited bundle AI into the plan price. Conversation quotas: Tidio's Growth climbs from $59 to $349 as volume grows, so budget for where you will be in six months, not where you are today. Branding removal: free plans almost always show the vendor's badge; removing it costs $29/month per site on Tawk.to and is plan-gated elsewhere.

Quick fact: the cheapest paid option with AI chatbot included is Crisp Mini at $45/month per workspace (AI credits included); the next is Tidio Starter at $29/month plus $39/month for Lyro — $68/month total.

What you lose at the cheapest tier

Cheap is not always enough. Here is what the budget tiers typically drop:

  • Chat history: Tawk.to keeps it free and unlimited; Chaport Free gives 60 days; LiveChat Starter gives 60 days; Olark's free tier has minimal history.
  • Real automation: free and entry plans usually offer only autoresponders and canned replies; true AI answering starts around $29-$58/month extra.
  • Channels: website chat is always included, but Messenger, Instagram, WhatsApp and SMS often sit on paid tiers.
  • Reporting: basic stats are free, but staffing prediction, SLA reporting and performance dashboards live on mid-tier plans.
  • Integrations: Zapier and CRM integrations are sometimes gated — Chaport puts Zapier on its $99 Unlimited plan, for example.

Frequently asked questions

Is Tawk.to really 100% free?

Yes, the core live chat is genuinely free with unlimited agents, unlimited chats and unlimited websites. You only pay for optional add-ons: $29 per month per site to remove the Tawk.to badge, $29 per month for AI Assist, and usage-based voice add-ons. Tawk.to also offers hired human agents from $1 per hour.

What is the cheapest live chat for a team of 3 people?

Tawk.to costs $0 with unlimited agents, which is unbeatable on price. If you want a paid tool, Chaport Pro is $29 per month (annual) and includes 4 operators, while Crisp Mini is $45 per month per workspace for up to 4 seats with unlimited conversations. A 3-agent team on LiveChat Team would cost $147 per month, and on Intercom Essential it would be $87 per month.

Do cheap live chat tools include chatbots?

Not always at the cheapest tier. Crisp includes AI automation credits on its $45 Mini plan, and Tidio bundles limited chatbot flows on its $29 Starter plan. Chaport puts chatbots on its $99 Unlimited plan, Olark offers AI chatbots only on custom Pro plans, and LiveChat's ChatBot starts at $52 per month as a separate product.

Which free live chat is best for ecommerce?

Chaport's free plan (2 operators, unlimited chats, 60-day history) is a strong start for small stores, and Tidio's free tier adds 50 conversations a month with a Shopify integration. Tawk.to is the most generous free option overall with no agent or chat limits. For stores that outgrow free plans, our guide to the best helpdesk software for ecommerce covers the next step up.

What hidden costs should I watch for in live chat pricing?

The biggest traps are per-agent pricing that multiplies with your team, AI chatbot fees billed separately from the base plan, conversation quotas that push you into higher tiers, and extra charges to remove vendor branding. Always multiply the headline price by your number of agents before comparing.

Conclusion

For a website on a budget, Tawk.to wins on pure price — unlimited free chat is hard to argue with. Chaport is the best cheap paid experience for small teams that want a clean widget, Tidio is the best value start for ecommerce stores that want AI, and Crisp is the best flat-rate deal once you have a real team. Per-agent pricing from LiveChat, Olark and Intercom looks modest until you multiply it out. Start free, measure what you actually need, and upgrade only at a real limit.
