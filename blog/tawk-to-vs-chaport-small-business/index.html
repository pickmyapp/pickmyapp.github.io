Tawk.to vs Chaport for small business compared (2026)

  • Customer Support
  • Published Oct 15, 2026
  • Updated: October 2026
  • 10 min read
  • By PickMyApp Research Team
Quick answer

Choose tawk.to if your priority is free live chat with no limits: the core product costs $0 with unlimited agents, websites, and chat history, plus ticketing and a knowledge base included. Choose Chaport if you want a cleaner upgrade path and built-in automation: its free plan covers 2 operators with unlimited chats, Pro starts from around $29 per month, and Unlimited (~$99 per month) unlocks chatbots, a knowledge base with an FAQ bot, and Zapier. Tawk.to wins on raw free value; Chaport wins when you want bots and predictable pricing without per-property add-ons.

Small businesses comparing live chat tools usually land on the same two names: tawk.to, the platform that gives its software away and charges for add-ons, and Chaport, the classic free-to-paid live chat built for small teams. Both have genuinely usable free plans — a rarity in this market — but they monetize in completely different ways, and those differences determine which one is cheaper and simpler for your business as it grows. This guide compares pricing, free-plan limits, chatbots, integrations, mobile apps, and setup, using vendor pages and reviews checked in October 2026. If you are weighing a wider set of options first, our roundup of the cheapest live chat software covers the whole budget end of the market.

What is tawk.to?

Tawk.to is a free live chat and customer messaging platform with no limits on agents, websites, or chat history. The core product — live chat widget, real-time visitor monitoring, a built-in ticketing system, a hosted knowledge base, and a lightweight contacts CRM — costs $0, and the company makes money from optional add-ons instead. That makes it one of the most generous free products in all of business software: a 20-person team running chat on five websites pays exactly the same as a solopreneur, which is nothing.

The add-ons are where the bill starts. Removing the "Powered by tawk.to" branding costs around $29 per month per property (annual billing; some sources report $39 on monthly billing). AI Assist, which can answer visitors automatically from your help center, starts at around $29 per month with a limited free Hobby tier. Video plus voice plus screensharing costs around $29 per month on annual billing. And tawk.to's most distinctive paid offering is Hired Agents — outsourced human chat agents trained on your business, from about $1 per hour, with published examples such as 24/7 coverage for around $728 per month. Every add-on is billed per property (per website or app), not per agent, so adding people is always free and adding sites is what costs money.

What is Chaport?

Chaport is a small-business live chat platform with a classic free-to-paid ladder and a strong focus on a clean agent experience. The free plan includes 2 operators, unlimited chats and websites, a limited chat history (recent reviews report 30 to 60 days depending on the source, so check the current page), basic widget customization, and web, desktop, and mobile apps. For a one- or two-person business, that free tier is genuinely enough to run chat day to day.

The paid tiers follow the familiar pattern. Pro starts from around $29 per month on annual billing (some review sources list $39 per month or $349 per year, so verify before buying) and includes 4 operators, unlimited chat history, auto-invitations, Facebook, Viber, and Telegram integrations, advanced widget customization, saved replies, pre-chat forms, detailed reports, an autoresponder, and REST plus JavaScript API access. Unlimited starts from around $99 per month on annual billing (about $129 monthly) with unlimited operators, and it is where the automation lives: chatbots, a knowledge base with an FAQ bot, teams, assignment rules, shared saved replies, custom fields, chat ratings, advanced reports, and Zapier integration. A 14-day free trial with no credit card required lets you test any tier.

How does tawk.to pricing compare with Chaport pricing?

The two platforms price like opposites. Tawk.to has one price — zero — and a menu of add-ons billed per website. Chaport has a three-rung ladder with per-plan features. Which is cheaper depends entirely on what you turn on.

When is tawk.to actually cheaper?

Almost always at the low end. A business with many agents and several websites that only needs human live chat, ticketing, and a knowledge base pays $0 on tawk.to, where Chaport's free plan would cap them at 2 operators. The math flips when you add tawk.to's paid extras: branding removal plus AI Assist on two properties runs around $116 per month, which is more than Chaport's Unlimited plan — and Chaport's price covers chatbots, FAQ bot, and unlimited sites in one flat bill.

Chatbots and automation

This is where the two diverge most clearly. Chaport's automation is rule-based: on the Unlimited plan you build chatbot flows, auto-invitations, assignment rules, and an FAQ bot that answers from your knowledge base. It is predictable and easy to reason about, and it is all included in the one plan. Tawk.to's automation story is its AI Assist add-on, which can answer visitors from your help center content and suggest replies to agents — more modern, AI-flavored automation, but billed per property on top of the free core. If you want classic no-code bots on a predictable bill, Chaport is simpler. If you want AI answers and are comfortable with add-on pricing, tawk.to's AI Assist is the more ambitious option.

Channels and integrations

Tawk.to's free core includes WhatsApp, Facebook Messenger, and Instagram messaging, which is generous for a $0 product. Chaport covers Facebook, Viber, and Telegram from the Pro plan upward. For integrations, Chaport has the clearer story: Zapier on Unlimited, a REST and JavaScript API from Pro, and named integrations with HubSpot, Salesforce, Zendesk, and Freshdesk. Tawk.to offers a JavaScript API and platform integrations, but its ecosystem feels thinner if you need chat wired deeply into a helpdesk stack. Teams that plan to graduate from chat to a full helpdesk should read our best helpdesk software guide and our Zendesk vs Freshdesk comparison before committing.

Which is easier to set up?

Both install the same way: paste one script tag before the closing body tag, or use the WordPress plugin, and neither needs a developer for a standard setup. The difference shows up after installation. Chaport's dashboard is deliberately simple — operators, canned replies, and widget settings are easy to find, and the free tier covers the basics with no configuration debt. Tawk.to's free tier is more powerful, but that power comes with more screens: triggers, shortcuts, departments, and routing rules take longer to set up, and several reviewers note the mobile app's notifications can be unreliable compared with desktop. For a non-technical owner who wants chat running in an afternoon, Chaport gets there faster. For an owner willing to spend an evening configuring things, tawk.to's free depth rewards the effort.

The honest trade-offs

Tawk.to's free-model trade-offs

Free does not mean frictionless. The "Powered by tawk.to" badge sits on your widget until you pay for branding removal, which looks unpolished on a premium brand's site. Add-ons are billed per property, so an agency or multi-brand business sees costs multiply with every site. The upsell surface is constant: AI Assist, video and voice, and Hired Agents are always one click away in the dashboard. And user reviews consistently flag two weak spots: basic reporting that lacks the depth teams want for measuring support performance, and mobile app notifications that sometimes arrive late, risking missed chats. None of this invalidates the free tier — it is still the best free chat deal in the market — but it means "free" comes with branding, upsells, and some rough edges.

Chaport's limits

Chaport's free plan is honest but narrow: 2 operators and a short chat history mean a growing team hits the ceiling quickly. The bigger limitation is that automation is gated behind the top tier — chatbots, the knowledge base, and the FAQ bot only exist on Unlimited at around $99 per month, so a small business that wants even simple bots pays the full top-tier price. Zapier, which many teams consider table stakes, is likewise Unlimited-only. And unlike tawk.to, Chaport has no built-in ticketing system with numbered tickets, priorities, and statuses; if your support model depends on tickets rather than chats, you will outgrow Chaport's scope sooner.

Quick fact: Both vendors run no-credit-card trials — Chaport offers 14 days on any paid tier, and tawk.to's core is free indefinitely, so you can run both side by side on a staging site before deciding.

Which should your business choose?

Choose tawk.to if

  • You want live chat at $0 with unlimited agents, sites, and history.
  • You need ticketing and a knowledge base without paying extra.
  • You run multiple websites and adding operators is free.
  • You might outsource overflow chat with Hired Agents at ~$1/hour.

Choose Chaport if

  • You want bots, an FAQ bot, and auto-invitations on one predictable bill.
  • You prefer flat-rate pricing where extra websites cost nothing.
  • You need Zapier and a REST API to wire chat into your stack.
  • You want the simplest dashboard a non-technical owner can run.

The dividing line is simple: if humans answer your chats, tawk.to is nearly unbeatable on value. If bots answer first, Chaport's Unlimited plan is the cleaner, more predictable buy. Solopreneurs and micro-teams can honestly start on either free plan; the decision gets easier once you know whether automation or unlimited human seats matter more to you.

Frequently asked questions

Is tawk.to really free?

Yes, the core product is free with no limits on agents, websites, or chat history. Tawk.to makes money from optional add-ons billed per property: removing branding (around $29 per month), AI Assist (from around $29 per month), video and voice calls (around $29 per month), and outsourced Hired Agents (from about $1 per hour).

How much does Chaport cost?

Chaport has a free plan (2 operators, unlimited chats and websites, limited chat history) and two paid tiers. Pro starts from around $29 per month on annual billing with 4 operators, and Unlimited starts from around $99 per month with unlimited operators, chatbots, a knowledge base with FAQ bot, and Zapier integration.

Which is better for chatbots: tawk.to or Chaport?

Chaport has the clearer chatbot story: rule-based chatbots and an FAQ bot are built into the Unlimited plan ($99 per month) with a straightforward no-code setup. Tawk.to offers AI Assist (from around $29 per month) that can answer from your knowledge base, but its chatbot capability is an add-on rather than a core tier feature.

Can I remove the branding from tawk.to's free chat widget?

Not for free. The free widget shows a "Powered by tawk.to" badge, and removing it requires the Remove Branding add-on, which costs around $29 per month per property. Chaport's free widget also carries branding, which is removed on the paid plans.

Which is easier to set up for a non-technical owner?

Both install with a single script tag or a WordPress plugin and neither needs a developer for a standard setup. Chaport's dashboard is simpler and its free tier covers most basics out of the box, while tawk.to's free tier is more powerful but its settings are spread across more screens and its automation takes longer to configure.

Conclusion

Tawk.to and Chaport prove that small businesses do not need to pay per-agent prices for good live chat — they just need to pick the pricing philosophy that matches how they work. Tawk.to is the value champion for human-powered chat: unlimited everything at $0, with ticketing and a knowledge base thrown in, and add-ons only where they earn their keep. Chaport is the cleaner choice when automation matters: a tidy dashboard, rule-based chatbots and an FAQ bot on one flat-rate plan, and pricing that does not multiply with every website you add. Run both free tiers for a week with real visitors; your chat volume and your tolerance for add-ons will tell you which camp you are in.
